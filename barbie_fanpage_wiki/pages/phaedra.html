Phaedra

Supporting Character (Muse)

About

Phaedra is another of the original Muses of Music. She is gentle and protective of the Diamond Castle and its music.

In the movie

Like Dori, Phaedra is turned to stone by Lydia after protecting the castle. She is freed when Lydia is defeated and reunites with Melody, Liana, and Alexa.

Relationships

  • Dori: Her fellow Muse of Music and close friend
  • Melody: The apprentice she helped teach
  • Lydia: The former Muse who betrayed her
  • Liana and Alexa: The heroes who save the castle

Fun facts

  • Heather Doerksen voices Phaedra, and also the waitress in the movie.
