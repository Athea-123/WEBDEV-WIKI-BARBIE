Ian

Supporting Character

About

Ian is Jeremy's identical twin, and also a singer and guitar player. He is kind and brave.

In the movie

Ian helps the girls on their way to the Diamond Castle and helps battle Lydia. By the end, he and Jeremy become real musicians.

Relationships

  • Jeremy: His identical twin brother
  • Alexa: The girl he falls in love with
  • Liana and Melody: Friends he helps on the quest

Fun facts

  • Noel Johansen voices Ian.
