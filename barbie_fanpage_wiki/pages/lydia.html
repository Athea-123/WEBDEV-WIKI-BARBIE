Lydia

Main Villain

About

Lydia is the main villain. She was once a Muse of Music like Dori and Phaedra, but she betrayed them because she wanted to rule the Diamond Castle for herself. She is jealous and power-hungry, and she uses spells to get what she wants.

In the movie

Lydia turns Dori and Phaedra to stone and sends Slyder after Melody and the girls. She uses a spell to control Alexa. In the end, Liana and Alexa use the Muses' instruments to defeat her.

Relationships

  • Slyder: Her dragon minion
  • Dori and Phaedra: Her former fellow Muses, whom she betrayed
  • Melody: The apprentice she wants to capture
  • Liana and Alexa: The heroes who stop her

Fun facts

  • Kathleen Barr voices Lydia.
