Sparkles

Supporting Character (Pet)

About

Sparkles is a cocker spaniel puppy. She is playful and adorable.

In the movie

Liana and Alexa find Sparkles lost on their journey and adopt her, and Liana names her. She becomes a loyal companion to the group.

Relationships

  • Liana and Alexa: Her new owners
  • Lily: Her puppy friend

Fun facts

  • Veena Sood voices both Sparkles and Lily.
