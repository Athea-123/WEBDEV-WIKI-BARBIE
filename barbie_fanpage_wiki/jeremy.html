Jeremy

Supporting Character

About

Jeremy is one of the twin musicians the girls meet in the village. He is friendly and talented, and he sings and plays guitar.

In the movie

Jeremy becomes a close friend to Liana and Alexa and joins their quest to the Diamond Castle. By the end, he and Ian become real musicians.

Relationships

  • Ian: His identical twin brother
  • Liana: The girl he falls in love with
  • Alexa and Melody: Friends he helps on the quest

Fun facts

  • Jeremy From voices Jeremy.
  • In the story Barbie tells, both twins are played by Ken.
