Dori

Supporting Character (Muse)

About

Dori is one of the three original Muses of Music who lived in the Diamond Castle. She is caring and wise, and she helped teach Melody.

In the movie

Lydia turns Dori to stone after hiding the castle and leaving Melody the key. She is freed when Lydia is defeated, and she joins Phaedra in welcoming Melody as a true muse and crowning Liana and Alexa princesses of music.

Relationships

  • Phaedra: Her fellow Muse of Music and close friend
  • Melody: The apprentice she taught
  • Lydia: The former Muse who betrayed her
  • Liana and Alexa: The heroes who save the castle

Fun facts

  • Nicole Oliver voices Dori, and also the maid in the movie.
