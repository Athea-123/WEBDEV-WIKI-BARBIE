Stacie

Supporting Character

About

Stacie is Barbie's younger sister. She is the listener of the story and the reason it is told.

In the movie

Stacie argues with her friend Courtney, so Barbie and Teresa tell her the story of Liana and Alexa to teach her about friendship. At the end, she apologizes to Courtney.

Relationships

  • Barbie: Her older sister who tells the story
  • Teresa: Barbie's friend who helps tell the story
  • Courtney: Her friend she argues with

Fun facts

  • Chantal Strand voices Stacie.
